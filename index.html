Coverage and limitations

Mercado Livre: 61 human-assisted page captures, 2,806 rows, 2,506 merged identity keys and 297 heuristic shortlist groups. Of those keys, 2,502 are catalog products and 4 are URL-only; none is an exact seller-listing identity. These are not 2,506 verified distinct copies.

Enjoei + OLX pilot: 3 pages (2 Enjoei, 1 OLX), 120 observations and 119 distinct listing IDs. Pilot shipping and fees were unverified at capture. The two OLX leads above now have 28 October guest-page shipping quotes, not checkout verification. This bounded sample is not market-wide coverage or evidence of rarity.

The automatic shortlist omits eligible singleton groups and can retain false positives. Human review must resolve title, platform, edition, authenticity, condition and offline/preservation value. Disc-only original copies are acceptable. Ratings enrichment and ML top-offer rechecks are still pending. The OLX pages show purchase buttons, not independently confirmed seller inventory.
